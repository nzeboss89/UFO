My Favourite Food

Rice and stew is my all-time favourite dish!

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Bell peppers
  • Garlic
  • Chicken or beef (optional)
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper

How to Prepare Rice and Stew

Here are the steps to prepare this delicious dish:

  1. Wash and chop the vegetables.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and add the vegetables.
  3. Cook until the vegetables are tender.
  4. Add rice and stir well.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
